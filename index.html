Questions owners ask us first.

What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is software that answers your business phone in a natural voice. A Loopforge agent picks up every call, day or night, answers common questions about hours, services and pricing, books, reschedules or cancels appointments directly in your calendar, and sends the caller an SMS or WhatsApp confirmation.

How much does an AI voice agent cost?

Every plan is quoted individually after a free consultation. Voice agents run on a monthly subscription that covers the calling platform the agent runs on, billed to you directly. Each plan includes a monthly call allowance sized to your call volume. If you go over it, calls go to voicemail instead of being billed extra.

How long does it take to go live?

Most projects go live in about two weeks. A starter website usually takes 5 to 7 days, and a voice agent or WhatsApp chatbot takes 1 to 3 weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back.

Which businesses is Loopforge built for?

Appointment-driven small businesses: clinics, dentists, gyms, salons, spas, studios and local service businesses. Recent projects include three dental clinics in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, a dessert café with online ordering, a bagel chain and a Mexican restaurant in the US, and a Houston realtor.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. Loopforge is based in India and works with clients worldwide, including live websites for businesses in Texas, Alabama and Louisiana. Indian clients are quoted in rupees with GST where applicable; international clients are quoted in US dollars.

Can the voice agent speak more than one language?

Yes. Multi-language calling is included in the Multi-location voice agent plan, and the Advanced WhatsApp chatbot plan supports multiple languages too.

Who owns the website or agent once it is built?

You do. Once a project is paid in full, ownership of the website files, voice agent configuration or chatbot flow transfers to you.
