# WhatsApp enquiry playbook (+91 91371 61472)

## How you'll know what a lead wants

Every WhatsApp button on the site opens a message that already says what the customer is interested in and where they clicked:

| Customer taps | Message they send |
|---|---|
| Contact: Booking website | Hi Loopforge, I'm interested in a booking website for my business. (from: Contact) |
| Contact: AI voice agent | Hi Loopforge, I'm interested in an AI voice agent for my business. (from: Contact) |
| Contact: WhatsApp chatbot | Hi Loopforge, I'm interested in a WhatsApp chatbot for my business. (from: Contact) |
| Contact: Not sure yet | Hi Loopforge, I'm not sure which service I need yet. Can you help? (from: Contact) |
| Services: "Ask on WhatsApp" | same service text, (from: Services) |
| Plans: "Ask about … plans on WhatsApp" | same service text, (from: Plans) |
| Floating "Chat on WhatsApp" button | opens a menu of the 3 services + "Not sure yet"; same service text, (from: WhatsApp button) |

Customers can edit the text before sending, so read it, but most won't.

## Set up once in the WhatsApp Business app

1. **Greeting message** (Business tools → Greeting message):
   "Thanks for messaging Loopforge. We reply within 2 hours, 10 am to 8 pm IST. Send your business name and city and we'll suggest a plan."
2. **Away message** outside hours: "We're offline right now and will reply by 11 am IST."
3. **Labels:** New, Website, Voice agent, Chatbot, Quote sent, Won, Lost. Label each new chat on your first reply.
4. **Quick replies** (type `/` in chat):
   - `/web`: what a booking website includes, then ask "What kind of business is it, and how do people book you today?"
   - `/voice`: how the voice agent answers and books, then ask "Roughly how many calls a day do you get, and how many go unanswered?"
   - `/bot`: what the chatbot handles, then ask "What do customers ask you most on WhatsApp?"
   - `/demo`: offer a free 20-minute call with two time options.
5. **Catalog:** add 3 items (Booking website, AI voice agent, WhatsApp chatbot) with a one-line outcome each.
6. **Business profile:** hours, loopforge.ai link, same wording as the site.

## Daily rule

Check chats labelled New at 10 am and 6 pm. Every new chat gets a label and a reply. Follow up once after 24 hours if there's no answer.

Only promise a reply time you can actually keep.
